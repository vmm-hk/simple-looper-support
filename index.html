How it works

TapRecord, close the loop, overdub, keep the overdub, or play again after a stop.
Double-tapStop. While recording or overdubbing it throws the take away.
HoldUndo the last overdub (hold again to redo). When stopped, clear the loop.
KnobDrag up or down to set the loop volume. Your live playing is not affected.

The full manual is in the app: Settings → How to use.

FAQ

It does not record anything.

Allow the microphone: iPhone Settings → Simple Looper → Microphone.

I hear a delay or feedback.

Use wired headphones or an audio interface. Bluetooth headphones add delay, and the input is muted on the phone speaker to avoid feedback.

Which MIDI footswitches work? (Pro)

Any Bluetooth or USB MIDI pedal that sends Note or CC messages, for example Boss FS-1-WL, AIRSTEP, M-Vave Chocolate, Morningstar MC series or Paint Audio MIDI Captain. Pair Bluetooth pedals in Settings → MIDI footswitch, then tap Learn and press the button. Keyboard-style page turners are not supported.

How do I get my loops out? (Pro)

Save the loop, then in Saved loops tap ••• → Export WAV or M4A and send it to Files, AirDrop or another app.

I bought Pro on another device.

Open Settings → Unlock Pro and tap “Restore purchase”. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription.

How do I reset the app?

Settings → Reset settings puts every setting back to default. Saved loops and Pro stay. Deleting the app removes everything.

Privacy

Simple Looper collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Microphone audio is processed on your iPhone and is never uploaded. Saved loops and settings stay on your device and only leave it when you export them yourself. Bluetooth is only used to connect MIDI footswitches. Purchases are handled by Apple; we do not see your payment details.
